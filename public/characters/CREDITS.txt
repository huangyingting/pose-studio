Built-in characters
===================

man.glb, woman.glb

  From "Universal Base Characters" (Standard, free version) by Quaternius
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters

  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0), Public Domain Dedication
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Changes: the Superhero_Male_FullBody and Superhero_Female_FullBody glTF models packed into
  binary glTF (GLB) without their textures (the app draws characters in its own styles), to
  keep the files small. The meshes and skeletons are as they came.

child.glb, heavy-man.glb, older-woman.glb

  Made with MakeHuman's base mesh, targets and Mixamo-named rig, through MPFB 2.0-b1 (the
  MakeHuman plugin for Blender), each shaped by MakeHuman's macro settings (a child of about
  eleven; a heavier man; an older woman), its targets baked, exported as GLB without textures.
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 (assets: LICENSE.ASSETS.md)
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md

  License: the MakeHuman / MPFB assets (base mesh, targets, rigs) are CC0 1.0 Universal, and so
  is what is made from them. (MPFB's program code is GPLv3; none of it ships with the app.)


aoi.glb

  "Aoi" (VRM avatar "girl") by Ueda, from https://github.com/uemegu/AnimeVRM.Motions
  (public/models/aoi.vrm).

  License: the avatar's own VRM 1.0 licence (https://vrm.dev/licenses/1.0/), as its file states:
  avatar use by everyone; personal, non-profit use only; credit required ("Aoi by Ueda");
  no excessively violent or sexual use, no political or religious use, no antisocial or hateful
  use. The original file may not be redistributed; a modified version may
  (modification: allowModificationRedistribution), under the same terms.

  Changes (scripts/vrm-character.py): its bones renamed as the app's skeleton's, its textures
  fitted within 1024 px, its spring bones left out. Its meshes, MToon materials, humanoid and
  licence are as they came.

mio.glb

  "Mio": aoi.glb (Aoi by Ueda, above) made over by scripts/characters/mio.py: silver-white hair
  and brows, grey-green eyes; a white bandeau (her T-shirt cut to a band) and white bikini bottom
  (her body's painted underwear recoloured); the holes VRoid left in her body under her clothes
  closed in her skin's colour; her cardigan and shorts taken off. Her body's shape, her face and
  her shoes are Aoi's; the same licence and credit apply ("Mio, after Aoi by Ueda").

adam.glb, aj.glb, brute.glb, dummy.glb, elizabeth.glb, erika-archer.glb, joe.glb, michelle.glb,
ninja.glb, ortiz.glb, paladin-j-nordstrom.glb, peasant-man.glb, pete.glb, remy.glb,
sporty-granny.glb, swat.glb, timmy.glb

  Characters from Adobe Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com), used under Adobe's terms for Mixamo
  (royalty-free use in personal, commercial and non-profit projects). "Dummy" is Mixamo's
  "Mannequin" (renamed: the app's own figure is the Mannequin).

  Changes (scripts/characters/mixamo.ts): converted from FBX to GLB by the app's own import, with
  only their colour textures (fitted within 1024 px), each vertex once, in metres; their bones
  renamed as the app's skeleton's. Their meshes, skeletons and painting are as they came.
